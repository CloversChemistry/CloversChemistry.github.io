## About Me
My name is Alexie Clover, and I am currently a PD chemist at LGC Axolabs in the North Bay Area. I grew up in sunny Arizona and after I graduated highschool I fled to the mountains of Flagstaff AZ where I went to NAU. I fell in love with organic chemistry my senior year and decided that I would continue my career somewhere without as much sunshine. I found myself in Bellingham Washinton at WWU getting my MS in chemistry. It was an adjustment to say the least going from AZ to WA, but I've grown fond of the rain. After my MS I landed somewhat in the goldilocks zone in California around the Bay.
